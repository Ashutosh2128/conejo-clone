# Dr. Maya Reynolds — Therapy Website

A calm, modern, and welcoming therapy website designed for **Dr. Maya Reynolds, PsyD**, a licensed clinical psychologist based in Santa Monica, California.

This project was created as a front-end development assignment focused on translating a therapist's profile and practice into a polished, client-focused website experience.

---

## 🌐 Live Website

**[Visit the Live Website](https://dr-maya-reynold.netlify.app/)**

**[View the GitHub Repository](https://github.com/Ashutosh2128/conejo-clone)**

---

## ✨ About the Project

The website is designed around Dr. Maya Reynolds' practice and the adults she supports.

The overall experience focuses on making visitors feel:

- Calm
- Comfortable
- Understood
- Safe
- Confident about taking the first step

The content and visual direction were created around the information provided in Dr. Maya Reynolds' therapist profile.

The website highlights her work with:

- Anxiety & Stress
- Trauma & EMDR
- Burnout & Perfectionism
- Panic
- Chronic Stress
- Self-worth & Confidence
- Relationships
- Past Life Experiences

It also communicates that Dr. Maya offers **in-person therapy in Santa Monica** and **secure telehealth sessions throughout California**.

---

## 🎯 Design Direction

The visual style was intentionally kept warm, minimal, and grounded to reflect the feeling of a comfortable therapy environment.

### Color & Mood

The design uses soft:

- Sage green
- Warm sand
- Clay
- Neutral tones

These colors help create a calm and welcoming first impression rather than making the website feel overly clinical.

### Typography

A combination of elegant serif headings and clean body text creates a balance between:

- Professional
- Personal
- Warm
- Modern

### Photography

Photography was selected to support the feeling of calm and comfort throughout the experience.

The website includes:

- Dr. Maya Reynolds' portrait
- Santa Monica imagery
- Therapy space imagery
- Actual office photographs provided for the project

## 🧩 Main Sections

### Hero

Introduces Dr. Maya and immediately communicates who she works with.

> **Feel more grounded in the life you're building.**

### About

Explains Maya's approach and speaks directly to thoughtful, high-achieving adults who may be struggling with anxiety, pressure, burnout, or overwhelm.

### Services

Highlights three primary areas of support:

- Anxiety & Stress
- Trauma & EMDR
- Burnout & Perfectionism

### Areas of Focus

Provides a broader view of the concerns Maya works with.

### How We Work

Explains Maya's warm, collaborative, and grounded approach to therapy.

Her work incorporates approaches such as:

- CBT
- EMDR
- Mindfulness-based practices
- Body-oriented techniques

### Honoring Your Story

A section focused on creating space for understanding past experiences while moving forward.

### Our Office

A custom section added specifically for this redesign.

It introduces Maya's Santa Monica office and gives visitors a visual sense of the calm, private, and comfortable environment.

It also highlights:

- In-person therapy in Santa Monica
- Secure telehealth throughout California
- A private and comfortable therapy environment

### Areas of Specialty

Provides a clear overview of Maya's main specialties and areas of support.

### Appointment

Ends the experience with a simple invitation to start a conversation.

## 🚀 Installation & Setup

### Clone the repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Ashutosh2128/conejo-clone.git
```

### Navigate to project directory

```bash
cd conejo-clone
```

### Install dependencies

```bash
npm install
```

### Run the application

```bash
npm run dev
```

### Open browser at

```text
http://localhost:3000
```
